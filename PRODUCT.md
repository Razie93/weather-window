# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 19, TypeScript, and Vite. The site runs locally and is deployable to Vercel as a static web app.

## Users

People checking the weather before leaving home, commuting, or planning the next several days. They need the current conditions and near-term changes at a glance, with enough detail to make a practical decision.

## Product Purpose

Weather Window provides live worldwide weather data in a calm, responsive interface. Success means a visitor can search or use their location, understand the current conditions, scan the next 24 hours, and compare the next seven days without sorting through a dense dashboard.

## Positioning

The interface behaves like a weather instrument: the page's light, color, and motion respond to the actual conditions while the forecast remains legible and task-focused.

## Operating Context

The product is used on phones and desktop browsers, often for a quick check under changing ambient light. Visitors may search for any location or allow browser geolocation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fetch live forecast and geocoding data from Open-Meteo without an API key.
- Show current conditions, a 24-hour trend, and a seven-day forecast.
- Support location search, browser geolocation, and Celsius/Fahrenheit switching.
- Handle loading, empty search results, denied geolocation, and network errors.
- Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion preferences, and responsive layouts.
- No user accounts, saved locations, severe-weather alerts, or production analytics in this version.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Weather Window. Copy should be direct, economical, and free of formulaic AI language. The experience should feel calm and instrument-like, not like a generic glass-card weather dashboard.

## Evidence on Hand

Open-Meteo provides the live forecast and geocoding data. No customer claims, testimonials, proprietary datasets, or commercial performance claims are available and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Put the next useful weather decision before secondary detail.
- Let conditions shape the atmosphere without reducing contrast or clarity.
- Keep interaction immediate: search, locate, and unit switching should respond without page reloads.
- Prefer a few clear forecast structures over a field of interchangeable cards.
- Explain errors in plain language and give the visitor a recovery action.
